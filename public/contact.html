Before your first class

What should I wear?

Shorts and a tank top or sports bra. Your skin is what grips the pole, so leggings make it harder. Bring a top layer for warm-up.

Anything I should avoid?

Skip lotion, oil and body cream on the day of class, and take off rings and bracelets. They make you slip and can scratch the pole.

I've never done this. Is that okay?

Yes. Intro to Pole and Intro to Spin are made for complete beginners. You don't need to be strong or flexible to start.

Do I need heels?

Not for most classes. Heels are optional in exotic classes; bring them if you have them.

What does it cost?

Each class is $100/month if it meets twice a week, or $75/month if it meets once a week. New students also pay a one-time $30 registration fee. See pricing.
