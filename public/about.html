Owner & instructor

Meet Sam

Samuel Montezco is originally from Laredo, TX and has been dancing, choreographing and performing for the past 30 years.

@trainbysam on Instagram

He is father to two beautiful children, Aiden and Sadie. He loves dance, but on his off time loves to just sit back and enjoy the little things in life.

He has taught all levels of dance, from children to adults. He currently teaches Ballet, Jazz, Hip-Hop, Tap, Modern, Flamenco, Belly Dance and Latin Ballroom at Sam's Personal Studio. He believes in teaching technique in its purest essence, but is adamant about developing all dance styles to innovative new heights.

How he teaches

“Know what you are doing.”

His teaching philosophy is simple: know what you are doing. There are many dancers who can execute steps but have no idea how or why. Mr. Montezco is highly detail-oriented about body placement, safe range of motion, and knowing why and where each movement comes from. His exceptional teaching skills are characterized by his relentless patience and precise eye for detail.

Choreography

He is known for original choreography that blurs all boundaries and is not afraid to explore new movements and concepts. He has choreographed for all levels of educational institutions, from elementary schools to universities. His work is thought-provoking and extremely unique. His great passion is to mix traditional and modern elements together, without losing respect for the specific dance genre.

On stage

Mr. Montezco's performance can only be described as awe-inspiring, supercharged with energy and emotion. He has performed at numerous festivals and concerts and is continually invited to perform in all types of venues. He is one of the rare dancers who can perform a multitude of dance genres and still maintain the integrity of each one's individual style and technique.

Fitness & personal training

Mr. Montezco has also been in the fitness industry for the past ten years as a group fitness instructor and personal trainer. His background in dance gives him a competitive edge that allows him to have an innate sense of how the body can be worked more effectively and safely. He has also developed many of his own fitness programs, such as Edge, Ascend, Center Pointe, and most notably Latin Groove and Hipnotik.

About the studio

The only original place to dance

Sam's Personal Studio is a dance studio and personal training center devoted to unlocking the amazing fitness potential of our students. With 19 years of teaching experience, we provide fun, fitness-oriented dance classes: pole dancing and belly dancing, from beginner classes to classes designed for experienced dancers. You can also book sessions with a personal trainer if you prefer individual advice and one-on-one attention.

If you've been searching for a way to get in shape that doesn't require endless crunches or hours on the treadmill, our pole and belly dance classes are a fun and exciting alternative. You'll tighten and tone while enjoying yourself in a friendly atmosphere. Our students come from all walks of life and are at all levels of fitness, so there's no reason to feel intimidated. Everyone is welcome at Sam's Personal Studio!
