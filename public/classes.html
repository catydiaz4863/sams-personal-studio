Classes

Find your class

Start with an intro class. When you're ready, the levels build on each other. Not sure where you fit? Text us and ask.

Pole

Beginner

Intro to Pole

Your first class. Grip, basic spins, walks and how to hold your own weight on the pole. No experience needed.

Beginner – intermediate

Pole Level 1 & 2

Build strength and string moves together: climbs, sits and your first combos.

Intermediate – advanced

Pole Level 3 & 4

Inverts, harder holds and transitions. For students who've finished Levels 1 & 2.

Spin pole

Beginner

Intro to Spin

Learn how to control a spinning pole: momentum, body position and staying on top of the dizziness.

Beginner – intermediate

Spin Pole 1 & 2

Spins, climbs and simple combos on the spinning pole.

Intermediate – advanced

Spin Pole 3 & 4

Inverts and flowing combos with the pole spinning underneath you.

Exotic & flow

All levels

Intro to Exotic

Slow, sensual movement, floorwork and pole flow. Heels optional.

All levels

Heel Flow

Choreography in heels: walks, body waves and floor transitions.

All levels

Polesque

Pole meets burlesque. Attitude, character and a lot of hair flips.

All levels

Chair & Floor

Choreography using a chair and the floor. A great place to build confidence.

Belly dance

All levels

Belly Dance

Hip drops, shimmies, isolations and short routines. No pole involved, all levels welcome.

Stretch & conditioning

All levels

Acro-Stretch

Flexibility and strength work for splits, backbends and cleaner lines on the pole.
