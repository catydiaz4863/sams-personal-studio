First time?

Here's how to start

  1. Pick a class

    Check this month's schedule. Brand new? Start with Intro to Pole or Intro to Spin.

  2. Text us to register

    Send a text to (555) 555-0123 with your name and the class you want. We'll save your spot.

  3. Show up and dance

    New students pay a one-time $30 registration fee. Wear shorts and skip the lotion, since skin grips the pole.
